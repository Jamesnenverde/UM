Product Description

The vivos X300 Ultra is a camera-first flagship co-engineered with ZEISS. It pairs two 200MP sensors (main and 3.7× telephoto) and a 50MP ultra-wide camera, all with optical image stabilisation, with a 6.82-inch 2K AMOLED display, the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, 512GB of storage and a 6600mAh battery with 100W wired and 40W wireless charging. It runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6 and is rated IP69 for water and dust resistance.
